What’s new in 1.0.3

Shortcuts where you need them

Create shortcuts for your virtual machines on your desktop or in the Start Menu.

Launch from the system tray

Access your configured shortcuts from the system tray and launch a machine when you need it.

Choose what happens next

Leave a VM running, save its state, or request a shutdown when its connection window closes.

Your VMs at a glance

The dashboard brings configured shortcuts, running VMs, VM state totals, active RDP proxy connections, and Hyper-V and Monitor availability together. Search your lists, check current states, and launch multiple selected machines.

Start a VM by connecting to it

Connect to a configured port on the host. Launcher Monitor starts the VM and forwards traffic to the guest. Set up remote connections.

Keep shortcuts in sync

Automatically create shortcuts for newly detected VMs, choose to be prompted, and clean up shortcuts when a VM is deleted. Choose your settings.

Follow recent activity

Search and filter VM, shortcut, and connection events, then open an event for details. Explore the Events page.

Recognize your machines

Guest OS details and icons make VMs easier to identify. Shortcuts keep the icon saved when they were created, and the compact tray menu keeps them close at hand.
